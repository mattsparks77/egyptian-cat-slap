Common questions

How do I play?

Players take turns putting one card face up on the pile. When the pile makes a slap pattern (two cards of the same rank in a row, for example), slap it. The fastest correct slap takes the whole pile. Face cards start a challenge: the next player has a few tries to play a face card of their own, or the challenger wins the pile. Win every card to win the game. The ? button in a match explains every rule that's switched on.

I bought something and didn't get it.

Close the game completely and reopen it while connected to the internet. The game asks the App Store for your purchases each time it starts. If your character pack is still missing, open the coin panel (the + beside your coins) and tap Restore Purchases. Still missing? Email us with the date of the purchase. You can also request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I need to pay to play?

No. Every mode is free, and every cat in the shop can be earned with coins from playing. Coin packs and character packs are optional shortcuts.

My coins or progress are different on another device.

Progress is saved to your player account. On iPhone and iPad, signing in to Game Center keeps the same account across your devices. Without Game Center, each install starts its own account.

How do I play with friends?

From the main menu choose Quick Play, open a private table, and share the code. Friends enter it to join. For a game on one phone, choose local play and pass the device around.

A match disconnected.

Online play needs a steady connection. Check your Wi-Fi or mobile data and start a new match. If it keeps happening, email us with the time it happened and whether you were on Wi-Fi.

How do I delete my data?

Email us from any address with your player name and ask for your data to be deleted. We'll remove your account and its saved progress within 30 days and confirm when it's done. See the Privacy Policy for what we store.
